Model: the third-party 27B ternary base model that Attis is adapted from, as a packed 2-bit GGUF file (7.17 GB).
- 15 prompt tokens + 4 generated: 3.812 s/token prompt, 4.705 s/token decode
- 30 prompt tokens + 32 generated: 3.521 s/token prompt, 4.386 s/token decode
No swap thrashing; the model was resident in RAM after loading; 45.2 C, not throttled.
